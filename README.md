A one-image front page summing up the world news of Saturday 21 November 2026: Russia's missile strike on Kryvyi Rih as the lead story, plus Mexico's capture of the FBI's most-wanted cartel boss, two tankers hit in the Strait of Hormuz, the deepening AI stock sell-off, the COP31 talks running past deadline, the EU's proposed Big Tech tax, Myanmar's rejection of the world court's genocide ruling and the UN's pledge of "killer robot" rules by 2028. The picture is drawn with Pillow by `draw.py` (stories and sources in `SOURCES.md`), packed into `main.ts` by `pack.mjs`, and written out verbatim when the program runs.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
